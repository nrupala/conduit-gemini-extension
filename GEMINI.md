# Conduit — Filings & Fundamentals

You have access to the Conduit MCP server: 24 read-only tools over U.S. SEC
EDGAR public filings. Use it whenever a question is about a U.S. public
company's filings or reported financials.

What it covers:

- Company lookup and profile, filing history, recent 8-Ks and amendments
- Latest 10-K / 10-Q facts: revenue, profits, EPS, assets, liabilities, cash, debt
- Computed ratios, revenue broken down by business segment
- Side-by-side comparison of up to five companies; ranking against every SEC filer
- Full-text search of filing text; insider (Forms 3/4/5) filings

Ground rules:

- Data comes from filings as reported to the SEC. If EDGAR does not report a
  figure, the tools say so — never estimate or fill gaps from memory when a
  tool can answer.
- Results are factual public-record data, not financial advice.
